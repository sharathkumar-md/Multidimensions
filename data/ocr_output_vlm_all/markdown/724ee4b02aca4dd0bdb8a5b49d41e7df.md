## 1.1 Advanced Precision Reduction Gearhead

The word "RV" is frequently used to describe a precise reduction gearbox that is used to increase torque while reducing the speed of a motor or other high-speed rotating shaft. A precision reduction gear RV generally comprises of a number of gears placed in a certain arrangement to decrease the input shaft's speed while boosting torque. When precise control of speed and torque is needed, a number of use RVs in robotics, machine tools, and other automated systems where accurate motion is essential; they are frequently utilized. Precision reduction gear RV can be used to change rotational direction, transmit power or motion between various system components, as well as decrease speed and increase torque.

# 1.3 AGV Drive Unit

An AGV (Automated Guided Vehicle) drive unit is a type of transportation system used to move materials within a facility or warehouse. AGVs are equipped with sensors and guidance systems allowing them to navigate through a facility and follow predetermined routes to transport materials between locations. The drive unit is part of the AGV that powers the vehicle's movement and controls its direction. Some AGVs may also be equipped with additional features such as load handling devices or pallet jacks to facilitate material movement and handling.

# 1.4 Servo Actuator

A servo actuator is a machine that directs the movement of a mechanical part using an electrical signal. Electrical signals regulate the motor, which drives the gear train, which drives the load-bearing component. By altering the electrical signal to the motor, the position or movement of the load-carrying unit can be accurately controlled. Automation systems and other applications that need for precise motion control frequently use servo actuators. In order to maintain a precise position or follow a predetermined path, the control system may, for instance, employ a sensor to measure the location of the load-carrying element and then modify the electrical signal to the motor using this information. They are frequently employed in difficult applications where precise motion control is essential due to their excellent accuracy, speed, rigidity, and reliability.
