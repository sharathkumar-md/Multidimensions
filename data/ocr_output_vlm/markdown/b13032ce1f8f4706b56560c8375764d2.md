- **Metal-Polymer Bearings**
  - Includes PTFE and thermoplastic based materials.
  - Common structure of steel backing bonded to porous bronze interlayer impregnated and overlaid with low-friction polymer bearing layer.
  - Applications include pumps and planetary gear sets.

- **Engineered Plastics Bearings**
  - Consists of injection-molded thermoplastic compounds with solid lubricants and fillers including non-abrasive fibers.
  - Offers lightweight solutions with homogenous bearing structure producible in almost any shape and form.
  - EP™44 offers good load and temperature resistance properties with good resistance to chemical attack from oils.
  - EP™64 offers superior resistance to load and higher temperatures with increased resistance to chemical attack from oils.

- **Bimetallic Bearings**
  - Comprising a steel backing and a bronze overlay without the addition of lead.
  - GGB-SZ bearings offer high load capacity and very good fatigue strength characteristics at high temperatures.

- **GGB Service and Support**

    - Design recommendations
    - Lifetime estimation
    - Test rig evaluation

- **DP4**
  - Widely mounted in shifting towers and shifting forks.

- **DP31**
  - Provides excellent resistance to corrosion, cavitation, and fatigue under cyclic loading conditions.

- **DTS10®**
  - Machinable for reduced tolerances with low friction and excellent resistance to fatigue, oil packaging, cavitation, and flow erosion.
  - Offers good behavior in frequent dry stop-start conditions.

# Applications

GGB's bushings and washers are widely used in manual transmissions MT, automated manual transmissions AMT, automatic transmissions AT, double-clutch transmissions DCT and continuously variable transmissions CVT and also in transfer cases and transaxles. Applications include:

- Rear output shaft bearing
- Main shaft bearing
- Reverse idler bearing
- Clutch releases
- Shifting actuators
- Differential gears
- Torque converters
- Sector bushings
- Accessory drives
- Solenoids
- Pumps
- Shifting towers
- Shift rails
- Planetary gear sets/carriers

# The GGB Advantage

GGB's compact and precision-made maintenance-free plain bearings are perfectly suited to operate in boundary and hydrodynamic lubricated conditions.

The advantages of collaborating with GGB include:

- **Improved Efficiency for Reduced Emissions**
  - Low coefficient of friction and breakaway torque, no stick-slip effect
  - High rotational speed and radial stability
  - High wear-resistant performance under a wide range of load, speed, and temperature conditions

- **Weight Reduction for Lower Fuel Consumption**
  - Weight reduction from the thin-walled bearing structure or light construction
  - Reduced power consumption from the low-friction bearing surface

- **Replace Needle and Roller Bearings**
  - Composite bearing overlay avoiding metal-to-metal contact and no moving parts to enable reduced noise
  - Economic and easy to assemble with no moving parts
  - Reduced material usage with the compact bearing design
  - Self-lubricating and tolerant to contamination and lubricant starvation for reliable operation
