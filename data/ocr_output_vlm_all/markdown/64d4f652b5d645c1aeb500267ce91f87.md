### HAKANOX
- Developed by HKO.
- Uses polycrylonitrile fibers (PAN).
- Thermally stabilized and oxidized.
- Suitable for environments up to 450°C.
- High priority in acoustic and thermal insulation.

### THERMO-E-GLASS
- Based on Thermo E-Glass products.
- Excellent electrical insulating properties.
- High thermal resistance.

### BASALT
- Made from basaltic rocks.
- Outstanding temperature range of -260°C to +800°C.
- Resistant to solvents, bases, and acids.

### HAKOTHERM®-800
- Made from calcium silicate fibers.
- Provides excellent protection against metal spillage, splashes, or liquid metal splashes.
