1.2 Precision Reduction Gearbox
RV are a type of gearhead or gear reducer, which is used to reduce the speed of an input shaft and increase the torque
output. RV gearheads typically consist of a series of gears arranged in a compact housing. The input shaft rotates the gears,
which reduces the speed and increases the torque output at the output shaft. The reduction ratio of an RV gearhead is
determined by the number of gears and their size.
RV-E gearhead is a solid shaft mounting type of planetary RV gearhead. It is known for its higher torque capacity and a lower backlash which

An AGV (Automated Guided Vehicle) drive unit is a type of  transportation system that is used to move materials within a
facility or warehouse. AGVs are equipped with sensors and guidance systems that allow them to navigate through a facility
and follow predetermined routes to transport materials between locations. The drive unit is the part of the AGV that
powers the vehicle's movement and controls its direction. Some AGVs may also be equipped with additional features, such
as load handling devices or pallet jacks, to facilitate the movement and handling of materials.

A servo actuator is a machine that directs the movement of a mechanical part using an electrical signal. Electrical signals
regulate the motor, which drives the gear train, which drives the load-bearing component. By altering the electrical signal to
the motor, the position or movement of the load-carrying unit can be accurately controlled. Automation systems and other
applications that need for precise motion control frequently use servo actuators. In order to maintain a precise position or
follow a predetermined path, the control system may, for instance, employ a sensor to measure the location of the
load-carrying element and then modify the electrical signal to the motor using this information. They are frequently
employed in difficult applications where precise motion control is essential due to their excellent accuracy, speed, rigidity
and reliability.
