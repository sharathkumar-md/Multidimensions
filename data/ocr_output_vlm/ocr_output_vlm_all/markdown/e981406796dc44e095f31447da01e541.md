# Hycomp Carbon Fibre Composite

Hycomp is a high-temperature polymer developed by Saint-Gobain Performance Plastics for critical application areas. It comes in two variants WearComp and FibreComp, both are featured with exclusive properties like lower friction coefficient(0.18/0.25), wear resistance with very high mechanical strength, and temperature up to 600°F.
